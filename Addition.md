Adding integers is simple
You will have two numbers, each number can have any amount of digits
Single digit addition is the easiest
You will take the first number, and the second number, and combine their quantities
For small numbers you can do this by taking the first number, for example 3
Then you can count up from that number as many places as the second number, for example 3
So if you start at 3, you can then go 4 (one jump), 5 (two jumps), 6 (three jumps) and then 7 which would be your fourth and final jump
